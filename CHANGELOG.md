# Changelog

## 1.2.1

- Fixed the Scuttle Bomb not chasing mobs or players after landing

## 1.2.0

- Added crafting recipes for the Fat Man, Mini-Nuke and Napalm Bomb

## 1.1.1

- Scuttle Bombs now chase players too, but never the player who threw them

## 1.1.0

- Added the Scuttle Bomb: throw it at a mob to blow it up, or land it nearby and it runs at the closest hostile mob within 16 blocks and explodes. Use it while it waits to pick it back up
- Redrew the Mini-Nuke as a finned bomb

## 1.0.4

- The Fat Man now shows a Mini-Nuke sliding into the muzzle as you load it

## 1.0.3

- The Demolition Bow and Bow of Levitation now animate while you draw them

## 1.0.2

- Napalm Bombs now set the ground on fire properly, and the flames keep burning for about 10 seconds

## 1.0.1

- New Arsenal pack icon

## 1.0.0

- Every TheCoberto weapon in one add-on: Fat Man, Explosive and Levitation bows and arrows, and the Creeper Bomb
- New Bee Bomb: bursts into a swarm of angry bees that attack everything nearby except the thrower, then fly off after 30 seconds
- Use instead of Rocket Launcher, Skeleton Variants and Bow Variants. Existing items carry over
