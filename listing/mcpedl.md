## Title
Coberto's Arsenal: Rocket Launcher, Bee Bombs and Explosive Bows

## Short description
A rocket launcher with Mini-Nukes, explosive and levitation bows, Bee Bombs that unleash angry swarms, and Scuttle Bombs that chase down your enemies.

## Introduction
Coberto's Arsenal puts every weapon I've made into one pack. Launch a Mini-Nuke and watch the mushroom cloud rise, blanket an area in fire with a Napalm Bomb, send mobs floating with a Bow of Levitation, or throw a Bee Bomb and let the swarm do the work. No experiments needed.

## Description
**Fat Man**

A rocket launcher that fires two kinds of ammo:
- **Mini-Nuke**: one huge explosion (radius 20) with a mushroom cloud.
- **Napalm Bomb**: a small blast, then sets everything within 8 blocks on fire. The flames burn for about 10 seconds.

Operators can run `/thecoberto:fatman` to choose whether explosions break blocks and whether Napalm Bombs start fires, so it's safe to use on servers.

Hold the ammo you want in your offhand to choose what to fire.

**Bows**
- **Demolition Bow** + **Explosive Arrows**: arrows explode on impact.
- **Bow of Levitation** + **Levitation Arrows**: whatever you hit floats into the air.

**Throwables**
- **Creeper Bomb**: a grenade that explodes on impact without breaking blocks.
- **Bee Bomb**: bursts into 6 angry bees that attack every mob and player nearby except you. They don't die when they sting and fly off after 30 seconds. Normal bees are never affected.
- **Scuttle Bomb**: a mine on legs. A direct hit explodes. If it lands on a block it waits, then runs at the nearest hostile mob or player within 16 blocks (never you) and explodes when it gets there. Use it while it's waiting to pick it back up.

**Crafting** (crafting table; everything is also in the creative inventory under Equipment)
- **Fat Man**: 3 iron ingots on the top row and 3 on the bottom, with tripwire hook, dispenser and redstone across the middle.
- **Mini-Nuke**: a diamond in the center, TNT on the four sides and iron ingots in the corners.
- **Napalm Bomb** (makes 2): TNT in the center, fire charges on the four sides and iron nuggets in the corners.
- **Demolition Bow**: bow in the middle, 3 TNT down the left column, 3 string down the right.
- **Bow of Levitation**: bow in the middle, 3 blaze rods down the left column, 3 blaze powder down the right.
- **Explosive Arrow**: feather, arrow and TNT in a row.
- **Levitation Arrow**: feather, arrow and blaze powder in a row.
- **Creeper Bomb**: tripwire hook top middle, iron ingot / gunpowder / iron ingot in the middle row, gunpowder / iron ingot / gunpowder on the bottom.
- **Bee Bomb**: same shape as the Creeper Bomb, with honeycomb in place of iron ingots.
- **Scuttle Bomb** (makes 2): redstone torch top middle, iron ingot / TNT / iron ingot in the middle row, iron nuggets in the bottom corners.

**Already using my other add-ons?**

Arsenal replaces Rocket Launcher, Skeleton Variants' bows and Bow Variants. Use it instead of those, not alongside them. The items are the same, so you can switch over in an existing world and keep everything you have.

## Installation
1. Download the .mcaddon and open it. Minecraft imports both packs.
2. Create or edit a world and turn on the Coberto's Arsenal behavior pack (the resource pack is added with it). No experiments needed.
3. Turn off Rocket Launcher, Bow Variants and Skeleton Variants in that world if you have them.

## Supported Minecraft versions
1.21.100 and newer
